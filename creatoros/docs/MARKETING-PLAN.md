# CreatorOS — Marketing Plan (UK/USA Launch)

**Product:** all-in-one creator monetization SaaS — bio pages, links, digital store,
courses, bookings, email marketing + automation, communities, analytics, AI credits.
**Site:** https://usecreatoros.co
**Pricing (USD/mo):** Free $0 · Starter $9 · Creator $19 (hero plan) · Pro $49 · Business $99.
Billing currency USD; one-time payments live via Cashfree, subscriptions open after KYC.
**Status:** pre-launch. Legal copy + subscription billing are the remaining blockers.
**Technology Partner:** Ridhyansh Tech Infra Private Limited.

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** "Create. Grow. Sell. Automate. All in One Place."
**Category:** Link-in-bio + creator store + email, in one login.
**Differentiators to repeat everywhere:**
1. One tool replaces 3–4 subscriptions (link page + store + email + bookings).
2. Sell from day one (products, courses, paid bookings, receipts).
3. Email list + automation built in (not an add-on).
4. Start free, upgrade when you grow, cancel anytime.

**Proof points to publish:** free plan limits, $19 vs buying 3 tools (~$40–60/mo),
receipt + analytics screenshots, creator case snippets as they arrive.

## 2. Who we target (ICP, in order)

1. **Solo creators & coaches (primary):** 1k–100k followers, sell or want to sell
   digital products, courses, 1:1 calls. Pain: tool sprawl + fees.
2. **Freelancers & consultants:** bookings + simple store + email list.
3. **Small agencies/teams (Business $99):** white-label, team seats, client pages.

**Anti-ICP (say no early):** enterprises needing SSO/SLA, physical-goods sellers
needing shipping, developers wanting API-first headless.

## 3. Funnel and conversion path

Landing (`/`) → Pricing (`/pricing`) → Register (free, consent checkbox) →
dashboard onboarding → first win checklist:
1. publish bio page, 2. add 3 links, 3. add 1 product or service,
4. import/collect 10 contacts, 5. send first email.
Upgrade triggers: hit free limits (1 page / 5 links / 10 contacts / 1k views),
custom domain, email automation, AI credits.

**North-star metric:** activated free workspaces (bio published + ≥1 product/service).
**Money metrics:** free→paid %, ARPU, MRR, churn, CAC payback.

Target guardrails (first 90 days): activation ≥25%, free→Creator ≥3%,
email deliverability ≥98%, site p95 < 2s, refund rate <2%.

## 4. Channels (ranked by effort/return for a solo founder)

1. **Founder-led short video (highest ROI):** daily 30–60s builds/tips on
   YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok. Format: problem → 20s build in
   CreatorOS → result. CTA: free link in bio.
2. **SEO content (compounds):** comparison + how-to posts
   ("link in bio for coaches", "sell digital products without a website",
   "CreatorOS vs Linktree", "email list for creators"). Resubmit sitemap,
   add `www`→apex redirect first.
3. **Email nurture (we own the list):** welcome series (day 0/2/5/9) → activation
   checklist, template drops, upgrade nudge at limit-hit. Dogfood our own tool.
4. **Partnerships/affiliates:** micro-creator affiliates (20–30% recurring),
   coach communities, template creators. Launch affiliate terms before scaling.
5. **Paid (only after funnel works):** small tests ($10–20/day) on Meta/YouTube
   retargeting site visitors + free users near limits. Kill fast if CAC > 6-month ARPU.
6. **Marketplaces/directories:** Product Hunt, AppSumo-style lifetime deal only if
   cash needed (note: LTD buyers churn/never upgrade — use sparingly), SaaS directories.

## 5. 90-day plan

**Days 0–14 — foundations (pre-launch):**
- [ ] Publish approved legal copy, remove `noindex`.
- [ ] Sitemap resubmit + `www`→apex redirect.
- [ ] Analytics events verified (signup, publish, product add, checkout).
- [ ] Welcome email series live in own product; password-reset deliverability proven.
- [ ] 5 template bio pages + 2 sample products/courses for demos.
- [ ] Record 10 evergreen short videos (batch).

**Days 15–45 — launch + learn:**
- [ ] Soft launch to waitlist/own audience; fix top 3 drop-offs.
- [ ] Publish 2 SEO posts/week; post 1 video/day.
- [ ] Recruit 10 affiliates; ship referral tracking.
- [ ] Weekly metric review: activation, free→paid, churn reasons.
- [ ] First 10 paying customers → testimonials + case snippets.

**Days 46–90 — scale what works:**
- [ ] Double down on best 1–2 channels; cut the rest.
- [ ] Start paid retargeting only if free→paid ≥2%.
- [ ] Launch Pro/Business outreach to agencies.
- [ ] Monthly changelog + template drops to fight churn.

## 6. Content pillars (rotate weekly)

1. Build-in-public (features shipped, numbers shared).
2. Creator money tips (pricing, funnels, email).
3. Templates & teardowns (good bio pages dissected).
4. Customer wins (screenshots, revenue shared with permission).

Posting cadence: 1 short video/day, 2 posts/week long-form, 1 email/week to list.

## 7. Launch checklist (marketing side)

- [ ] Landing + pricing copy final, footer legal links live.
- [ ] OG tags/social preview verified for `/` and `/pricing`.
- [ ] Free signup works end-to-end on mobile.
- [ ] Support inbox + response-time promise published.
- [ ] Refund + cancellation flow tested (billing page, email confirm).
- [ ] Status/health page or contact path if checkout fails.

## 8. Budget sketch (pick one)

- **$0/mo (organic only):** time = 1–2 hrs/day content + SEO. Slow, sustainable.
- **$300/mo:** domain/tools + $200 retargeting tests.
- **$1k+/mo:** add creator sponsorships ($50–200/micro-post) + bigger paid tests
  only after organic free→paid is proven.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

- Subscription billing blocked (KYC) → sell one-time products/courses first;
  position subscriptions as "coming at launch".
- Single-founder support load → templates + docs deflect; office-hours support promise.
- Deliverability (new domain) → warm up email, SPF/DKIM/DMARC via Brevo, monitor bounces.
- Chargebacks/abuse → rate limits + audit logs already in product; watch refund reasons.

## 10. What to decide now

1. Monthly paid budget: $0 / $300 / $1k+?
2. Launch date and first channel (video vs SEO vs affiliates)?
3. Affiliate commission (suggest 30% recurring, 12 months)?
4. Who answers support in week 1, and response-time promise?
