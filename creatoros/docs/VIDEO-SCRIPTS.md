# CreatorOS — Founder Video Script Pack (10 × 30–45s)

Format: 9:16 vertical, hook in first 3 seconds, CTA at end. Speaking style: founder-led, plain, no teleprompter voice. Post 2–3×/week. Every script ends with one clear CTA → `/pricing` or free signup, and uses the `#UseCreatorOS` build-in-public tag.

Pillars: **build-in-public** (1–4), **money/creator tips** (5–7), **teardowns** (8–9), **wins/proof** (10).

---

### 1. Build-in-public: "The 4 subscriptions I cancelled"
- **Hook:** "To build this, I cancelled 4 subscriptions."
- **Body:** Bio tool, store, calendar, email — $54/month before. One login now. Show the stack comparison on screen (4 logos → 1).
- **CTA:** "Free plan, no card. Link in bio → sign up. #UseCreatorOS"

### 2. Build-in-public: "One page, four jobs"
- **Hook:** "Your bio link should do 4 jobs. Mine does all 4 on one URL."
- **Body:** Show the bio page: product card with price, book-a-call button, email capture, store. Tap through each with 1-line value.
- **CTA:** "Build yours in an hour, free. Link in bio."

### 3. Build-in-public: "What I'd tell my past self about selling digital"
- **Hook:** "I started selling 18 months late because I thought I needed a website."
- **Body:** The "website first" myth → one store page + payment + email beats a perfect site that ships in 3 months.
- **CTA:** "Your first product can be live tonight. Free. Link in bio."

### 4. Build-in-public: "The audience you rent vs the audience you own"
- **Hook:** "Please clap for the algorithm. You're renting your audience."
- **Body:** Followers vs email list; show the built-in email tab in the dashboard; one welcome-automation shot.
- **CTA:** "Capture your first email free. Link in bio."

### 5. Money/creator: "$19 vs the $54 stack"
- **Hook:** "Most creators pay for 4 tools doing 1 job: bio."
- **Body:** Walk the math grid (bio + store + calendar + email = $54). $19 creator plan = same jobs, one login. Highlight the Creator plan card on screen.
- **CTA:** "Pricing is public. Link in bio → /pricing."

### 6. Money/creator: "Bookings + store on the same link"
- **Hook:** "Two buttons every creator should have beside each other: Buy and Book."
- **Body:** Show the booking card with availability + product card with price on one page; "a booked call is revenue with a name attached."
- **CTA:** "Free page. Link in bio."

### 7. Money/creator: "Deliver your product, not your patience"
- **Hook:** "Digital products fail when delivery is manual."
- **Body:** Receipt email, instant download, branded receipt URL — cut to the receipt screen.
- **CTA:** "Sell something tonight. Free. Link in bio."

### 8. Teardown: "Linktree but it sells"
- **Hook:** "I'm not cool about it: your link in bio is a list, not a store."
- **Body:** Honest teardown. Links vs store+booking+email; the "tap → redirect → pray" leak; same bio slug with a store front end.
- **CTA:** "Swap it in an hour. Free. Link in bio."

### 9. Teardown: "The 3-page funnel for creators"
- **Hook:** "Creators don't need a funnel. They need a shop."
- **Body:** Landing → Pricing → product/booking. Show how the store page, a booking service and a course enroll all route through one public page.
- **CTA:** "Free plan → link in bio."

### 10. Wins: "First Week of UseCreatorOS — numbers"
- **Hook:** "(timestamp) Official build-in-public progress: live, 2 users, 6 bio pages, 0 products sold."
- **Body:** Honest numbers today, dashboard shots, invite the audience to beat us. Start the "first sale" public counter.
- **CTA:** "Track the build with us. #UseCreatorOS"

---

### Posting rhythm
- Weeks 1–2: post #1, #2, #5, #8 (foundation + teardown mix).
- Weeks 3–4: post #3, #6, #9, #7.
- Week 5+: #4, #10 + repeat winners; iterate hooks based on 1st-second retention in the editor.

### Filming notes
- Batch-record 4 scripts per sitting (same outfit/lighting).
- Use a 3-panel screen share: phone bio page, dashboard, pricing.
- Hook line goes in the first 3 seconds and must be said, not text-only.
- End card: "CreatorOS — Create. Grow. Sell. Automate. All in One Place."
