# CreatorOS — US/UK Ad Copy & Targeting Kit

Use with **Google Ads → Video campaign** (Shorts/In-feed). Also reusable for Search
and Meta later. Character limits noted.

## Positioning
One workspace for your whole creator business — bio pages, store, courses,
bookings, email and analytics. Start free, no card required.

- US angle: replace 9 tools, start selling today.
- UK angle: less chaos, one tidy platform.

## Custom-intent audience keyword themes
Load `keywords.csv` (column `keyword`). Highest priority themes: `competitor`
and `intent`. Keep the audience tight (<= 30 keywords) so intent stays sharp.

## Targeting summary (Google Ads)
- Locations: United States, United Kingdom
- Languages: English
- Demographics: 18-44, all genders
- Devices: Mobile (Shorts inventory)
- Placements: YouTube Shorts, YouTube search partners (optional)
- Audiences: Affinity "Media & Entertainment/Online video";
  In-market "Business Services"; plus the custom-intent list above

## Budget & bidding
- Start $10-20/day, split 50/50 US/UK
- Bidding: Maximum CPV
- Kill any creative under ~1% CTR after ~$300 spend
- Success metric: cost per free signup (link Google Ads conversions to the site)

## Notes
- US/UK paid traffic needs USD/GBP pricing live on the site before scaling.
- Keep the first 3 seconds of every ad as the hook (the videos already do this).
