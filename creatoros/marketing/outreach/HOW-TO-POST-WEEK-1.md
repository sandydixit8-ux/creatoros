# Week 1 runbook - how to post the eight touches

This is the click-by-click version of `free-channels-week-1.md`. The schedule, the copy and the
rules live there; this file only answers "what do I actually do at 10:00".

Posting has to come from your own accounts. There is no automation here on purpose: these
communities ban scripted posting, and a real person answering a real question is the whole point.

Verified 2026-10-30: `https://usecreatoros.co/?utm_source=outreach&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=us_uk_first10`
returns 200, and `/auth/register` returns 200, so links used in a later DM are not broken.

## 1. The loop for every touch (5 steps, about 12 minutes)

1. **Open the place** using the link in the table below (or the group's own search).
2. **Pick one thread**: posted in the last 48 hours, question in the title, under ~20 comments,
   and nobody has already posted the fee maths. Skip anything older than a week.
3. **Read the thread before pasting.** If your message would answer a different question, rewrite
   the first line so it answers theirs.
4. **Paste the copy below**, change nothing else. No link on a first touch.
5. **Log the row** in `tracker-2026-10-30.csv` (section 4). Posting without logging is how the same
   person gets messaged twice.

If the subreddit or group removes the comment, do not repost it. Log `skipped`, note the reason,
and move to the next place.

## 2. The eight touches

Times are market-local. On 2026-10-30 the 10:00 UK slot has already passed by the time this
runbook was written, so either post it late today or move it to Sat 31 Oct 10:00 UK - log whichever
you do.

### Touch 1 - Fri 30 Oct, r/EtsySellers (message 3)

Open: https://www.reddit.com/r/EtsySellers/search/?q=fees&restrict_sr=1&sort=new

Pick a thread where someone is asking what the fees actually are, not a rant with no question.

> Quick decision tree, based on what you're optimising for:
>
> - **Fastest to first sale, no audience:** Etsy or Gumroad. You pay 10-12% for discovery.
> - **Cheapest per sale at low volume:** Payhip free plan, 5% + card fees.
> - **Cheapest at volume:** Payhip Pro ($99/mo, 0%) once you're past ~£2k/month.
> - **You want store + courses + bookings + email in one place:** an all-in-one platform, free tiers exist.
>
> One thing people miss: fixed fees. A £5 download loses ~18% on Etsy because £0.36 of it is fixed. Price at £15+ or bundle, and the percentage drops by a third.

### Touch 2 - Fri 30 Oct 18:30 UK, Facebook UK Etsy Sellers (message 5)

Open the group, search the group for `fees` or `payout`, sort by recent. Comment on a post about a
fee or payout surprise.

> Worth checking your fee breakdown per order rather than the monthly total - Etsy bills the listing fee separately from the transaction and processing fees, so the total looks smaller than it is.
>
> On a £20 sale you're at about £2.56 all in. On a £6 download it's £1.02, which is 17% - that's why cheap digital listings feel like they barely pay. Bundling three files into one listing is the quickest fix, because you pay the fixed fees once.

### Touch 5 - Mon 2 Nov 09:00 ET, r/Entrepreneur (message 4)

This one names CreatorOS and discloses it. Only post it in a thread that is actually comparing

> Fees as of now, on a £20 sale with UK card processing:
>
> - Gumroad: 10% + 50¢ → you keep ~£17.60 (30% if the sale comes through Discover)
> - Payhip free: 5% + Stripe → ~£18.50
> - Payhip Pro: $99/mo, 0% → breakeven around £1,980/month
> - Etsy: 6.5% + 4% + 0.48% + £0.20 + £0.16 → ~£17.44
>
> So Gumroad and Etsy cost about the same per sale; Payhip is cheaper; the subscription only wins at volume. Everything else is about what you get beyond checkout - email, courses, bookings. Buying those separately is $30-100/month, which usually costs more than the fee difference.
>
> (Disclosure: I build CreatorOS, which is in the all-in-one camp. The numbers above are the platforms' own published rates either way.)

## 3. When someone replies

Only now, and only after their reply, do you send a follow-up. **One follow-up maximum.** No reply
means stop - two unanswered messages is spam.

**They give numbers:**

> With your numbers: [X] sales at £[Y] = £[Z]/month. Etsy takes about £[A]. Direct checkout would be about £[B], so the gap is £[C]/month.
>
> The honest catch is traffic - if Etsy search is where most of those buyers come from, moving today costs you more than the fees. If you want, I'll sketch what a 90-day parallel run would look like for your catalogue.

**They ask what you use:**

> I build CreatorOS - bio page, store, courses, bookings and email in one place, free plan, no card. Full disclosure: it's mine, so take the recommendation with that in mind.
>
> Free to try here if it's useful: https://usecreatoros.co/?utm_source=outreach&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=us_uk_first10
>
> Happy to answer anything about the fee maths either way - that part isn't platform-specific.

Never name CreatorOS before they ask. Never send the link in a first reply.

## 4. Logging - one row per touch

File: `creatoros/marketing/outreach/tracker-2026-10-30.csv`. Nine columns, fixed order:

`date,slot,place,target_thread,message_no,status,reply,outcome,next_step`

`status` is one of: `ready` (not posted yet), `posted`, `replied`, `no reply`, `skipped`, `planned`
(the 5 Nov review row). Nothing else.

Practical way to edit it without breaking the CSV: open in Notepad, change the row in place, save as
UTF-8. Fields containing a comma must stay in double quotes; keep the header line untouched.

Before posting (row 2, for example):

```
2026-10-30,10:00,r/EtsySellers,"fees - sort New - reply with the fee maths",3,ready,,,post then log
```

After posting it, replace the row with:

```
2026-10-30,18:45,r/EtsySellers,"https://www.reddit.com/r/EtsySellers/comments/abc123/",3,posted,,,check replies 2026-10-31 10:00
```

When the person answers:

```
2026-10-31,10:00,r/EtsySellers,"https://www.reddit.com/r/EtsySellers/comments/abc123/",3,replied,"asked for their average price",sent fee breakdown,DM the 90-day outline on 2026-11-02
```

If the comment was removed or the thread was wrong:

```
2026-10-30,19:05,r/EtsySellers,"removed by automod - new account",3,skipped,"mod removal",,retry 2026-11-02 with an older account
```

Two things to keep clean: put the **thread URL** in `target_thread` once posted (so a follow-up does
not need a search), and keep `reply` short - a quote of what they asked, not the whole message.
