## 4. New copy - US variants

### A. Reddit - "where do I sell digital downloads?" (r/smallbusiness, US)

> Depends what you are optimising for:
>
> - **Fastest first sale with no audience:** Etsy or Gumroad. You pay roughly 12% for discovery.
> - **Cheapest per sale at low volume:** Payhip free plan, 5% plus card processing.
> - **Cheapest at volume:** Payhip Pro ($99/mo, 0%) once you are past about $2k/month.
> - **Want store, courses, bookings and email in one place:** an all-in-one platform, free tiers exist.
>
> The bit people miss is fixed fees. A $20 sale on Etsy is about $2.35 all in (about 12%), but a $5
> download is about $0.93 (about 18%), because $0.45 of it does not shrink with the price. Price at
> $15+ or bundle three files into one listing and the percentage drops by about a third.

Why it works: real numbers, no pitch, ends with something actionable.

### B. LinkedIn - comment on a US creator-economy post

> The ownership point is the one that compounds. On a marketplace, every repeat customer costs the
> full fee again, and you cannot email them before a launch. A $20 sale nets about $17.65 on Etsy
> versus about $19.12 on direct card processing - small per order, but it is the email list that
> changes the second launch, not the 10%.

### C. X - reply to a public fee complaint

> The 6.5% is only the transaction fee. Add 3% + $0.25 processing and $0.20 listing and a $20 sale
> is about 12%. On a $5 download it is about 18%, because the fixed fees do not scale.
>
> Bundling and a $15+ entry price fixes more of this than switching platforms does.

### D. Facebook - US creator / small business group value post

> Did the maths on marketplace fees vs your own checkout, in case it is useful here.
>
> 40 sales a month at $20 = $800. Etsy, all fees in: about $94/month. Own checkout at standard card
> processing: about $35/month. Difference: about $59/month, about $708/year.
>
> But that is only a saving if you can replace the traffic Etsy sends. If ads or content cost more
> than $708/year to bring the same buyers, the marketplace is still doing its job.
>
> What has worked for people: keep the marketplace live, move the three best sellers to your own
> store, and send all social traffic to the store for 90 days. Then decide with data.

Check: $20 sale on Etsy = $1.30 transaction + $0.85 payment + $0.20 listing = $2.35; 40 sales =
$94. Direct: $20 x (2.9% + $0.30) = $0.88 per sale; 40 = $35.20. Both rounded in the post.
